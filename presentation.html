Section 1 · Introduction & Context PROBLEM & OBJECTIVES

Problem Statement, Background & Objectives

Addressing Municipal Service Latency and Administrative Verification Gaps

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Civic Redressal Friction

Conventional municipal grievance portals suffer from severe lack of evidence integrity, missing GPS data, and manual ticket triage, resulting in 7 to 14 days of administrative inaction before reaching ward engineers.

BUSINESS & TECHNICAL CONTEXT

Fragmented Public Systems

Citizen reports are siloed across disconnected helplines, social media complaints, and paper registers. Emergency dispatches lack automated geographic proximity calculation to active trauma facilities.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

What Success Looks Like

Zero-friction citizen capture with tamper-proof location verification, automated department dispatch under 4 seconds, strict 48-hour resolution enforcement, and rapid sub-3 minute emergency SOS triage.

Core Goal: Bridge the operational disconnect between citizen reporting and local government action through automated multi-agent routing and cryptographic evidence integrity.

Section 3 · Approach & Methodology ARCHITECTURE & DESIGN TRADE-OFFS

System Design, Tools & Architectural Trade-Offs

Why This Technology Stack Was Chosen Over Conventional Alternatives

FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE

React 18, Vite & Zustand

Selected for instant sub-3 second loading, zero framework bloat, and predictable reactive state synchronization across mobile screens and desktop command centers without unnecessary re-renders.

GEOSPATIAL ENGINE

Leaflet & OpenStreetMap

Chosen over Google Maps API to eliminate recurring licensing costs and remove commercial API key requirements and watermarks, ensuring 100% reliable sovereign municipal uptime.

AUTOMATION GATEWAY

n8n Cloud Webhook Mesh

Chosen over hardcoded backend microservices for rapid extensibility, visual audit trails, autonomous scheduled social crawlers, and instant webhook connectivity to government SMTP and CRM systems.

Section 6 · Testing & Quality VERIFICATION STRATEGY

Testing Strategy, Edge Cases & System Reliability

Ensuring Operational Integrity Across Real-World Environmental Conditions

TESTING METHODOLOGY

Multi-Layer Quality Assurance

  • Unit Testing: GPS distance mathematics and HMAC hash generators
  • Integration Testing: n8n cloud webhook dispatches and email relay verification
  • Cross-Device Validation: Mobile responsive views (360px, 390px, 430px) and desktop command displays
EDGE CASES HANDLED

Resilient Fault Tolerance

  • No Camera / Blocked Permission: Fallback to native device file capture or simulated incident presets
  • Low-Accuracy GPS: Multi-tiered Tamil Nadu regional coordinate resolution
  • Intermittent Connectivity: Local queue caching with state synchronization on reconnection
KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Ongoing Refinements

  • Requires device GPS sensors for high-accuracy ±4m positioning
  • Email relay deliverability dependent on municipal spam filters (mitigated via verified SMTP relays)
  • Offline video streams fallback to file-based capture

Section 8 · Challenges & Learnings RETROSPECTIVE ANALYSIS

Obstacles Overcome & Engineering Key Learnings

Technical and Operational Challenges Navigated During Platform Implementation

OBSTACLE 01

Hardware Camera Lifecycles

Browser permission interruptions and React hook re-render cycles caused camera streams to stop unexpectedly. Solved by decoupling stream tracking into mutable refs and providing immediate native fallbacks.

OBSTACLE 02

Tile Provider Watermarks

CartoCDN basemap services began enforcing commercial API keys and displaying watermark overlays. Solved by migrating to open-access OpenStreetMap tiles with zero key dependencies.

WHAT WE WOULD DO DIFFERENTLY

Architectural Retrospective

Prioritize open-source GIS basemaps from day one and implement native camera input fallbacks alongside WebRTC streams earlier in the development lifecycle to streamline desktop simulation.
