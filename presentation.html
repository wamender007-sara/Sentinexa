Problem Statement CIVIC OPERATIONS BOTTLENECK

Structural Municipal Service Inefficiencies

Current Operational Bottlenecks in Citizen Grievance Redressal and Emergency Response

SECTION 01

Absence of Geotagged Proof

Complaints frequently lack exact GPS coordinates and photographic verification, resulting in field verification delays, duplicate submissions, and jurisdictional ambiguity across municipal wards.

SECTION 02

Manual Routing Bottlenecks

Grievances pass through several administrative levels and manual sorting desks, leading to standard response latencies of 7 to 14 days before reaching the responsible ward engineer.

SECTION 03

Siloed Emergency Services

Medical trauma, police, and civic emergency systems operate in isolated silos without unified geospatial mapping, slowing down critical multi-agency coordination during severe accidents.

Strategic Impact: Replacing manual ticket routing with automated geographic verification and direct webhook orchestration reduces initial response delay by over 90%.
